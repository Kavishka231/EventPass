# Database

PostgreSQL is the permanent source of truth. Flyway migration `V1__create_core_schema.sql` creates users, venues, venue seats, events, per-event seat inventory, bookings, booking items, payments, tickets, and hashed refresh tokens. `V2` adds the transactional outbox and idempotent-consumer ledger; `V3` permits cancelled-seat resale; `V4` scopes booking idempotency and stores request fingerprints; `V5` adds the durable payment lifecycle; `V6` adds durable refunds; and `V7` adds outbox delivery status, persisted retry scheduling, and a due-delivery partial index. Hibernate runs with `ddl-auto=validate` and Open Session in View is disabled.
